Abstract

Computational protein design requires sampling 3D conformations that respect physical translational and rotational symmetries. We introduce an educational, mathematically consistent, pure PyTorch geometric diffusion architecture operating on protein alpha-carbon (\(C\alpha\)) coordinates. Primary focus is given to conditional structural inpainting of internal loop segments (5–15 residues) anchored by rigid tertiary contexts, complemented by unconditional coarse-grained trace generation. The model employs an \(SE(3)\)-equivariant graph neural network (EGNN) denoiser and an accelerated DDIM reverse sampler with strict context preservation. Benchmarked on held-out crystallographic structures with zero parent-chain leakage, the model achieves consecutive bond length deviation within \(0.34\text{ \AA}\) of ideal peptide spacing (\(3.81\text{ \AA}\)), maintains a low non-bonded steric clash rate (\(3.32\%\)), and samples structurally diverse ensembles (\(3.40\text{ \AA}\) pairwise diversity). We also formalize the closed-loop computational self-consistency paradigm (\(scRMSD\)) bridging backbone diffusion, inverse folding via ProteinMPNN, and structure prediction via ESMFold.

1. Introduction & Methodology

Generative deep learning on protein geometry requires architectures that respect the symmetries of 3D physical space: global translations and rotations in \(SE(3) = SO(3) \ltimes \mathbb{R}^3\).

We organize the research into two complementary tracks:

  • Track A (Model Trained from Scratch): A compact EGNN denoiser operating on \(C\alpha\) coordinates with conditional loop inpainting as the primary task.
  • Track B (Pretrained Generation & Self-Consistency Ecosystem): Analysis of industry-standard tools (RFdiffusion, FrameDiPT, ProteinMPNN, ESMFold) evaluated via computational self-consistency (\(scRMSD < 2.0\text{ \AA}\)).

2. Mathematical Foundations & Equivariance

Let a protein chain be represented by \(C\alpha\) coordinates \(\mathbf{X} \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times 3}\). Under rotation matrix \(\mathbf{R} \in SO(3)\) and translation vector \(\mathbf{t} \in \mathbb{R}^3\), coordinates transform as \(\mathbf{X}' = \mathbf{R}\mathbf{X} + \mathbf{t}\). A coordinate-predicting neural network \(f\) satisfies:

$$f(\mathbf{R}\mathbf{X} + \mathbf{t}) = \mathbf{R}f(\mathbf{X}) + \mathbf{t}$$

Displacement vectors and predicted noise \(\hat{\mathbf{\epsilon}}_\theta\) are translation-invariant but rotate equivariantly:

$$\hat{\mathbf{\epsilon}}_\theta(\mathbf{R}\mathbf{X} + \mathbf{t}) = \mathbf{R}\hat{\mathbf{\epsilon}}_\theta(\mathbf{X})$$

In the EGNN layers, pairwise squared distances \(d_{ij}^2 = \|\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{x}_j\|^2\) remain invariant under \(SE(3)\). Invariant scalar messages \(m_{ij}\) scale relative difference vectors \((\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{x}_j)\), ensuring that the accumulated displacement transforms as a true rotation-equivariant vector.

6. Track B: Pretrained Generation & Self-Consistency

In modern computational structural biology, de novo backbones are validated through the Analysis-by-Synthesis loop:

$$\text{Backbone Sample } \mathbf{X} \xrightarrow{\text{ProteinMPNN}} \text{Sequence } \mathbf{S} \xrightarrow{\text{ESMFold}} \text{Refolded } \mathbf{X}_{\text{pred}} \implies scRMSD = \text{RMSD}(\mathbf{X}, \mathbf{X}_{\text{pred}}) < 2.0\text{ \AA}$$

Standard ProteinMPNN strictly requires complete backbone heavy atoms (\(N, C\alpha, C, O\)). Providing coarse-grained \(C\alpha\) traces requires either a dedicated \(C\alpha\) checkpoint or prior all-atom reconstruction (e.g., BBQ or Pulchra).

7. Limitations & Technical Notes

  • Coarse-Grained Geometry: The model generates \(C\alpha\) traces. Downstream molecular dynamics or all-atom parameterization requires sidechain reconstruction.
  • Reflection Invariance: Distance-based EGNN operates in \(O(3)\). Distinguishing L-amino from D-amino enantiomers requires chiral reference frames.
  • Superposition Alignment: Evaluated loops were aligned strictly via fixed context atoms. Aligning loops independently masks boundary placement errors.
